AgrowCart is a user-friendly and visually appealing online agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers, vendors, and customers. The platform facilitates the purchase and sale of agricultural products such as cereals, vegetables, fruits, spices, dairy, poultry, gardening tools, pulses, dry fruits, and more. The project serves as a prototype for an e-commerce website catering to agricultural needs, aiming to make farming and related business operations more accessible and efficient.
